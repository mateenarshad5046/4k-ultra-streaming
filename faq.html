Evaluation Phase Details

How does the lP.TV evaluation phase operate?

+

Simply send a request via WhatsApp, and we will issue a temporary access line for your preferred media application. There is zero financial commitment upfront: you can test live broadcasting, local sports feeds, and overall stability on your own network. You only transition to a paid plan if you are completely satisfied with the performance.

Is it genuinely a cardless process?

+

Absolutely. We do not require any billing information, credit cards, or upfront deposits to initiate your test. You evaluate the network entirely risk-free and decide on a subscription strictly on your own terms.

What is the duration of the testing window?

+

Your temporary credentials provide full network access for a comprehensive 24-hour period. This gives you ample time to stress-test the servers during peak evening matches and verify picture clarity across different channels.

Which local networks and sports broadcasts are included?

+

You gain unrestricted access to our complete lineup, which includes major networks like CBC, CTV, and Global, alongside premium sports channels like TSN and Sportsnet, plus dedicated regional feeds and French-language broadcasts such as RDS.

Which hardware platforms are compatible?

+

Our lP.TV network is highly versatile and works smoothly on Amazon Fire TV Sticks, Android set-top boxes, Apple TV, modern Smart TVs (Samsung, LG, Sony), smartphones, and tablets through standard media players like Smarters Pro and TiviMate.

What are the minimum internet speed requirements?

+

To ensure a flawless viewing experience—particularly for 4K resolution and high-speed live sports—we strongly recommend a stable broadband connection of at least 15 to 25 Mbps.

Are there any hidden contracts after the evaluation?

+

There are zero hidden fees, contracts, or forced auto-renewals. Once your testing period concludes, you can manually select a fixed-term CAD package ranging from 3 to 24 months, all backed by a solid refund policy.

How quickly can I get set up?

+

The entire setup process usually takes less than 10 minutes. Send a quick message to our support team on WhatsApp, and we will immediately provide your login details along with an easy-to-follow installation guide.
