In a modern Canadian household, it is rare for everyone to agree on watching the exact same program. While one family member wants to catch live hockey in the living room, someone else might want a movie on a bedroom Firestick or a news broadcast on a tablet. Utilizing your 24-hour evaluation window to test multiple concurrent connections ensures you select a plan capable of supporting your entire home without frustrating bottlenecks.

1. Assessing Household Demands and Screen Count

Before requesting your test credentials, take a quick inventory of how many active screens your family typically runs during peak evening hours. Some members only require a single connection plan, while busy households benefit tremendously from bundles supporting two, three, or more simultaneous streams.

Knowing your household's exact requirements ahead of time helps you maximize your trial period, allowing you to focus your evaluation directly on the tier that matches your lifestyle.

2. Executing the Multi-Connection Stress Test

When you message our support team on WhatsApp for your trial, mention that you would like to evaluate multi-screen performance. Once you receive your lines, fire them up across different devices simultaneously during a busy evening hour.

Observe whether opening extra streams introduces any latency spikes, audio desyncs, or buffering loops on your primary television. A robust, professionally maintained server network will handle multiple concurrent connections effortlessly, delivering crystal-clear performance across every active screen.

Once your 24-hour stress test concludes successfully, navigating to our Plans page makes choosing the right option straightforward. Our pricing tiers feature built-in discounts when adding extra connections to your subscription.

By verifying your concurrent screen capacity during the trial phase, you guarantee that every member of your household enjoys uninterrupted entertainment from day one of your paid subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Can standard trial lines support more than one screen?

+

Standard trial lines default to a single connection for server protection. However, you can easily request a temporary multi-screen test line by letting our support team know via WhatsApp.

Does running multiple streams require faster internet bandwidth?

+

Yes. Each active HD or 4K stream consumes its own share of broadband data. We recommend an internet speed of at least 50 to 100 Mbps if your household plans on running multiple screens concurrently.

Can I add more connections to my subscription later?

+

Absolutely. You can easily upgrade or adjust your screen count whenever your subscription term is up for renewal, or by chatting with our support staff.
